Members who leave the library want to close their account, so please add `closeAccount(memberId, today)` to the loan service. After that the member shows the status "closed" and can't borrow anymore. Any small fees they still owe should be cleared when they close, because chasing a few dollars costs us more than it brings in. A lot of leavers also ask us to forget them, and it would be nice to see in the reports who left.
